# Scene 2: "Ketenangan" (tranquility)

A hidden grotto at dusk turning to night. Deep violet-blue and indigo, with cyan light falling through a skylight in the cave roof. An old valley keeper sits on a flat rock and fishes in a still pond beside a glowing waterfall. A paper lantern behind him is the only warm light: it gives his back an orange rim and sets a red accent against the cool palette.

- **Canvas**: every file is `2560 × 2304` (`viewBox="0 0 2560 2304"`). The visible 16:9 frame is y 432–1872, with 432 px of bleed above and below.
- **Focus**: `focusX .38` on desktop and `.30` on mobile. On a 390 × 844 phone the visible slice is about x 576–1242. The elder and his rock sit inside that slice. The waterfall shows only at the left edge, and the turtle is cut off.
- **Light**: the cool key light comes from the opening at (760, 360), which is the same origin as `GODRAYS.tranquility`. The warm fill comes from the lantern at about (1150, 1414). Far shapes are lighter, bluer and lower in contrast.
- **Copy-safe zone**: x 1500–2420, y 650–1450 (right side). It is covered by the shadowed right cave wall (`cave-mid`), which uses only very low-contrast planes. Nothing bright or busy goes there. Hanging moss stays above y 650, and plant tops stay low and dim.
- **Code-drawn layers (not painted)**: god rays (`tranquility-godrays`), bats (`tranquility-bats`) and the WebGL waterfall (`tranquility-waterfall`, drawn over the rect x 360–620, y 300–1680).
- **Technical rules**: all files are static SVG. They contain no `<animate>`, `<script>`, CSS or filters. Each file is under 300 KB, and every id is prefixed with the file name.
