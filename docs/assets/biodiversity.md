# Scene 3 — "Biodiversitas" (biodiversity) — layer sheet

A humid river valley at blue hour, seen from under a forest canopy. A chital stag drinks at the river's edge in front of a waterfall on the right. The palette is teal and violet: teal `#3fe0c0` accents (moss rims, leaf rims, shallow water, bounce light), violet `#5b2c9a` shadows, and a soft peach glow from the upper middle (≈ x 1480, y 600–760) that lines up with `GODRAYS.biodiversity` (origin 1500, 320).

- **Canvas:** 2560 × 2304. The 16:9 frame is y ∈ [432, 1872]. The 432 px above and below are bleed.
- **Focus:** `focusX` .60 / mobile .66. At 1440×900 the visible canvas x is about 139–2443. At 390×844 (mobile .66) it is about 1244–1909, and that slice holds the glow, the full deer, the edge of the cliff and the bottom flowers.
- **Copy-safe zone:** the copy sits on the LEFT, x 0–1150, y 650–1450. That area only has dark, low-contrast cedar silhouettes, mist and a darkening gradient (`bdLeft`, `ffLeft`). No flowers or highlights are placed there.
- **Waterfall rect (WebGL):** x 1930–2140, y 380–1560. `forest-mid` never covers it: its left pillar stops at x ≤ 1928 and its right wall starts at x ≥ 2142.
- **Top bleed:** dense dark canopy (backdrop + forest-mid fringe). It continues from the tranquility → biodiversity bridge.
- **Bottom bleed:** the end of the experience. The riverbank, big leaves and hydrangeas continue down to y = 2304.
- **Static files:** all SVGs are static. There is no `<animate>`, CSS animation, `<script>` or filter. Glows are radial gradients. Each file is under 200 KB.

### Deer construction (for replacement art)

- **Style:** flat 3-tone cel (base `#93524a`, shadow `#5a2e4a`, highlight `#b8704f`) plus a peach rim (`#f4b892`, core `#ffd8bc`) on every edge that faces the sun behind the deer: topline, neck crest, brow/poll, ear tops and the back edges of the antlers. No outlines; edges come from value contrast. Teal bounce (`#3fe0c0`) from the river runs along the belly and the backs of the cannons. White belly and throat are shaded lavender (`#cdb6c8` / `#8e7aa8`).
- **Body box:** torso x 1446–1799, y 1336–1518 (withers 1518, 1336). Hooves rest on the bank: near fore x 1462–1493 and near hind x 1726–1757 at y 1697; far fore x 1418–1446 and far hind x 1688–1716 at y 1687.
- **Legs:** the near fore is vertical and weight-bearing (forearm → carpal knee at y ≈ 1596 → cannon → fetlock knob with dewclaw → sloped pastern → cloven hoof). The far fore steps forward with a slightly flexed knee. The hind legs run thigh → stifle (≈ 1714, 1470) → gaskin → hock pointing back (near hind point at 1780, 1582; far hind at 1746, 1580) → cannon → hoof.
- **Head:** one head (wedge skull, nasal stripe, almond eye with preorbital gland, dark nose pad, pale chin) authored in a local frame. The poll is at 0,0, the muzzle tip at (−131, 3) and the jaw toward +y. Each pose places it with `translate(poll) rotate(θ) scale(1.06)`. The antlers are lyre-shaped with three tines each: a long brow tine just above the burr, and a terminal fork. They taper from 18 px (near) / 16 px (far) at the burr and sit on coat-coloured pedicles.
- **Coat:** cream spots in rows offset from the topline (they follow the barrel curvature), arcs around the hip, sparse small spots on the shoulder, a white lateral line, and lilac spots where the coat is in shadow.
- **Ground:** a soft cast shadow falls toward the viewer (radial gradient centred at 1600, 1702, 250 × 30), with darker AO ellipses under each hoof and teal grass blades in front of the hooves. The body's reflection would land on the opaque bank, so only the drinking head reflects (in the rest pose, clipped to open water).
